
Destination: Goa

Goa is a coastal destination in western India. It is known for beaches, Portuguese heritage, markets, seafood and nightlife.

Popular attractions:
- Baga Beach: A popular beach destination.
- Fort Aguada: A historic Portuguese-era fort.
- Basilica of Bom Jesus: A historic church and heritage site.
- Dudhsagar Falls: A major waterfall attraction.
- Anjuna Flea Market: A popular shopping market.

Suggested travel themes:
- Beach holidays
- Heritage sightseeing
- Nature trips
- Shopping
- Nightlife

Travel planning notes:
- A five-day trip can include beaches, heritage sightseeing, nature and shopping.
- Travel times and attraction access depend on the season and transport arrangements.
- This document is general destination information, not a confirmation of opening hours, ticket prices or availability.
