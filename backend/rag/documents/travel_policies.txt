
TravelOps AI - Demonstration Travel Policies

1. Pricing
All prices in the demonstration dataset are illustrative estimates.
They are not live quotes, confirmed supplier rates or booking prices.

2. Hotel accommodation
Hotel prices are stored per room per night.
The demonstration budget calculator assumes up to two travelers per room.
Actual room occupancy and availability must be confirmed with the supplier.

3. Transportation
Transportation estimates are based on the origin and destination entries available in the dataset.
If a route is unavailable, the system must report that no estimate is available.
It must not invent a transport price.

4. Food
Food estimates are calculated per person per day.

5. Activities
Activity costs are stored per person.
Free attractions have an estimated cost of zero.
Do not claim that guided tours, tickets or other services are included unless the source explicitly says so.

6. Availability
The system does not have live hotel, flight, transport or attraction availability.
All quotations must clearly state this limitation.

7. Budget
The total estimate must be calculated using the budget calculator.
Do not rely on language-model arithmetic for financial totals.
